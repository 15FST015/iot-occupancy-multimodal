# Declaration of Generative AI — Template untuk Naskah

Sesuai panduan Elsevier (Energy and Buildings — "Declaration of generative AI in
scientific writing") dan prinsip etika workflow publikasi (dokumen user).

## Pernyataan (masukkan di naskah, bagian Declaration/Acknowledgment)

> **Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process**
>
> During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools
> (large language models) to support literature synthesis, experiment
> scripting, statistical analysis, and language editing. The authors reviewed
> and verified all results, claims, citations, and numbers against the
> original experimental outputs and primary sources; AI tools were not used
> to generate or fabricate data, and they are not listed as authors. After
> using these tools, the authors reviewed and edited the content as needed
> and take full responsibility for the content of the publication.

## Klausul tambahan (untuk Methods bila diminta)

> **Use of AI in the research process.** AI-assisted tools were used for
> computational scripting and literature organization. All experiments,
> data analyses, statistical tests, and figures were produced and verified
> by the authors; the experimental protocol, split design, and final
> decisions were made by the authors.
